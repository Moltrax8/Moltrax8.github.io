Hack.lu CTF 2026

Hack.lu CTF is an internationally recognized, jeopardy-style cybersecurity competition organized annually by the FluxFingers team alongside the Hack.lu security conference in Luxembourg. For the 2026 edition, I competed alongside ROTACTF (part of the Atatürk University ROTA club). We secured 49th place globally, finishing with 1,779 points across 22 challenge solves. Our run required heavy lifting in Reverse Engineering, successfully clearing complex challenges including Vaulty, VeryMildHidden, and License Validation Suite. It was a highly technical event that thoroughly tested our reversing capabilities and team coordination.
